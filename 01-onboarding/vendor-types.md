# Vendor types

Not every vendor is the same. OPVS recognises three broad categories, and the differences affect your approval flow, your default trust tier, and what scope of skills you can publish.

This page explains:

- **Internal teams** — OPVS staff publishing under `@opvs-ai/*`
- **Partner teams** — established collaborators with their own scope (today: `@spideriq`, `@di-atomic`)
- **Third-party developers** — independent builders publishing to a vendor scope of their own
